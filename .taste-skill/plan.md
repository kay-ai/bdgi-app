# BGDI website revamp: page plan (tasteskill 2.5)

Kind: service (page type service, site-kind other). Free debate, public speaking and STEM programmes for secondary school girls in Oyo and Osun; buyers are students, parents, schools, volunteers and donors. Main action: Join a programme.

Deal: hero `split` (deal.mjs), nav `nav-mega`, footer `footer-dark` (re-dealt: `footer-visit` needs a public street address BGDI has not given), close `close-photo`, rhythm `surface-pairs`, buttons `arrow`, compositions `bleed` and `strip`. Palette and fonts NOT dealt: the client asked to keep BGDI's cream, ink, gold and green and the current serif/sans pairing (client instruction wins over VI-02). Radius `round` (consumer, children). The 3D journey stays (client asked for it); it breaks motion.md "nothing moves on scroll" for kind other and is reported.
